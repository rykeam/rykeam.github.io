A bit about me

I'm a data analyst based in Austin with a background in Texas state government. Most days I'm writing SQL, wrangling data into shape, and explaining what it means to the people who need to act on it.

I also lead a team, so I care a lot about clear documentation, good standards, and reports that executives actually read.
