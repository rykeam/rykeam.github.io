Notes
  • The timeline is by month. Bars and milestones come from the WSSA Cyclical Task Timeline workbook (2025–2031). "Listed start/end" keeps the dates exactly as written in that workbook's Start and End columns.
  • Year labels follow the five-year planning cycle: 2025 is Year 4 and 2026 is Year 5 of the 2027 cycle; 2027 starts Year 1 of the 2032 cycle.

  • Edits save for everyone with the link and show up on other devices within about 30 seconds.
